### TAB 2.1: ELECTIONS (Default Tab)

#### What is an Election?
| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Definition | "An election is a formal process where citizens choose their representatives through voting. It's the bridge between the people and the government." |
| Key Point | "In India, we elect representatives at 3 levels: National (Lok Sabha), State (Vidhan Sabha), and Local (Panchayat/Municipality)" |

#### Types of Elections in India
| Type | Description | Frequency | Who Votes |
|------|-------------|-----------|-----------|
| **Lok Sabha** | National Parliament (Lower House) | Every 5 years | All citizens 18+ |
| **Vidhan Sabha** | State Legislative Assembly | Every 5 years | State residents 18+ |
| **Rajya Sabha** | Parliament (Upper House) | Every 2 years (1/3 members) | Elected MLAs |
| **Presidential** | President of India | Every 5 years | Electoral College (MPs + MLAs) |
| **Local Body** | Panchayat / Municipality | Every 5 years | Local residents 18+ |

#### Election Commission of India (ECI)
| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Role | "Independent constitutional authority that conducts all elections in India" |
| Established | 25th January 1950 (celebrated as National Voters' Day) |
| Head | Chief Election Commissioner + Election Commissioners |
| Key Functions | Voter registration, Election schedule, Model Code of Conduct, Result declaration |
| Website | https://eci.gov.in |

#### Key Terms Glossary
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Constituency | Geographic area that elects one representative |
| Candidate | Person contesting the election |
| Manifesto | Document outlining party's promises |
| Ballot | Method of casting vote |
| EVM | Electronic Voting Machine |
| VVPAT | Paper slip that confirms your vote |
| NOTA | "None of the Above" option |
| Model Code of Conduct | Rules parties must follow during elections |

|---------|---------|

|---------|---------|

### TAB 2.3: CONSTITUTION

#### Constitutional Foundation of Elections

##### Fundamental Rights Related to Elections
| Article | Right | Description |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| **Article 326** | Universal Adult Suffrage | Every citizen 18+ has right to vote |
| **Article 324** | Election Commission | Establishes ECI as independent authority |
| **Article 325** | No Discrimination | No person excluded from electoral roll based on religion, race, caste, sex |
| **Article 327** | Parliament's Power | Parliament can make laws regarding elections |
| **Article 329** | Bar to Court Interference | Courts cannot interfere in electoral matters during elections |

##### Key Constitutional Amendments for Elections
| Amendment | Year | Change |
|-----------|------|--------|
| 61st Amendment | 1989 | Voting age reduced from 21 to 18 |
| 73rd Amendment | 1992 | Panchayati Raj - local self-governance |
| 74th Amendment | 1992 | Municipality elections |

##### Constitutional Bodies
| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| Election Commission of India | Conduct elections |
| Supreme Court | Electoral disputes |
| High Courts | State election petitions |

##### Constitutional Values in Elections
| Value | How It's Protected |
|-------|-------------------|
| Secrecy | Secret ballot - no one knows your vote |
| Equality | One person, one vote - equal value |
| Freedom | Free to vote for anyone or NOTA |
| Independence | ECI independent of government |

## PAGE 3: PROCESS (`/process`)

### Purpose
Step-by-step guide on **how to vote** - from registration to casting your vote.

### Sections & Data

#### 3.1 Header
| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Title | "Your Complete Voting Guide" |
| Subtitle | "From registration to casting your vote - everything you need to know" |

#### 3.2 Before Election Day

##### Step 1: Check Eligibility
| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Age | Must be 18 years or above on qualifying date (January 1st of the year) |
| Citizenship | Must be Indian citizen |
| Residence | Must be ordinary resident of the constituency |
| Not Disqualified | Should not be of unsound mind, declared by court |

##### Step 2: Register as Voter
| Method | Steps |
|--------|-------|
| **Online** | Visit voters.eci.gov.in → Fill Form 6 → Upload documents → Submit |
| **Offline** | Get Form 6 from BLO/ERO office → Fill & attach documents → Submit |
| **App** | Download Voter Helpline App → Register through app |

| Required Documents |
|-------------------|
| Proof of Age (Birth certificate, 10th marksheet, passport) |
| Proof of Address (Aadhaar, utility bills, bank statement) |
| Passport size photograph |

##### Step 3: Get Voter ID (EPIC)
| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| What | Electoral Photo Identity Card |
| Why | Primary ID for voting (though not mandatory) |
| How | Automatically sent after registration approval |
| e-EPIC | Download digital version from voters.eci.gov.in |

##### Step 4: Find Your Polling Station
| Method | Steps |
|--------|-------|
| Online | voters.eci.gov.in → Search Electoral Roll → View polling station |
| SMS | Send EPIC<space>EPIC_NUMBER to 1950 |
| App | Voter Helpline App → Know Your Polling Booth |

#### 3.3 On Election Day

##### Step 5: Documents to Carry
| Accepted ID Proofs (Any One) |
|------------------------------|
| Voter ID Card (EPIC) |
| Aadhaar Card |
| Passport |
| Driving License |
| PAN Card |
| Bank/Post Office Passbook with photo |
| MNREGA Job Card |
| Pension document with photo |
| Service ID of Central/State Govt |
| Student ID (for students) |
| Property document with photo |
| Smart Card issued by RGI |

##### Step 6: At the Polling Station
| Stage | What Happens |
|-------|--------------|
| **Queue** | Stand in line at your designated booth |
| **Verification** | Officer checks your ID and marks name in register |
| **Indelible Ink** | Left index finger marked with ink |
| **Ballot Unit** | Proceed to EVM booth |

##### Step 7: Casting Your Vote (EVM Process)
| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Enter the voting compartment (private booth) |
| 2 | Look at the EVM - shows candidate names, symbols, and buttons |
| 3 | Press the button next to your chosen candidate |
| 4 | You'll hear a beep and light will glow |
| 5 | VVPAT will show a paper slip for 7 seconds |
| 6 | Verify your vote on the slip |
| 7 | Exit the booth |

##### NOTA Option
| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| What | "None of the Above" - last option on EVM |
| When to Use | If you don't want to vote for any candidate |
| Impact | Your vote is counted but doesn't go to any candidate |
| Right | You have full right to use NOTA |

#### 3.4 After Voting

##### What Happens Next
| Stage | Timeline | What Happens |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| Voting Ends | Evening of polling day | Booths close, EVMs sealed |
| Strong Room | Same day | EVMs stored securely |
| Counting Day | Usually 3-4 days later | EVMs opened, votes counted |
| Results | Same day as counting | Winners declared |
| Oath | Within days | Elected members take oath |

#### 3.5 Your Rights as a Voter
| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Right to Vote** | Cannot be denied based on religion, caste, gender |
| **Secret Ballot** | No one can ask who you voted for |
| **NOTA** | Right to reject all candidates |
| **Accessible Voting** | Special facilities for disabled voters |
| **Paid Leave** | Employers must give leave on voting day |
| **Assistance** | Can request help if unable to vote independently |

#### 3.6 Common Questions
| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| What if my name is not in the list? | Apply for inclusion using Form 6 before deadline |
| Can I vote without Voter ID? | Yes, with any of the 12 approved photo IDs |
| What if EVM malfunctions? | Inform presiding officer, machine will be replaced |
| Can I change my vote? | No, once button is pressed, vote is final |
| What if VVPAT shows wrong candidate? | Immediately inform officer, your vote will be recorded on paper |

#### 3.7 Visual Process Flow
```
Register → Get Voter ID → Find Booth → Election Day →
Carry ID → Queue → Verification → Ink Mark →
Enter Booth → Press Button → Verify VVPAT → Exit → Done!
```
